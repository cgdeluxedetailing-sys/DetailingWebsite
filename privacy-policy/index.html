Privacy Policy

How information provided to CG Mobile Detailing is handled.

When you contact or book with CG Mobile Detailing, information such as your name, contact details, vehicle information, service requirements and appointment details may be received.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, arrange and deliver services, manage appointments, process payments and communicate with customers about bookings or services.

The website may link to or use third-party services such as online booking, payment, social media and hosting providers. Those providers may have their own policies and terms.

Security and retention

Reasonable steps are taken to protect customer information and information is retained only as reasonably required for business, legal or service purposes.
