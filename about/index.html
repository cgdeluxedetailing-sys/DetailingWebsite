THE BUSINESS

Built around the work.

CG Mobile Detailing is locally operated, mobile by design and focused on giving customers a straightforward way to get their vehicle detailed.

OWNER OPERATED

Connor Garvey · Founder

CG Mobile Detailing was started with the aim of building a real local business around quality vehicle care. The business story is straightforward: a genuine interest in cars, pride in the finished result and a mobile model that makes detailing easier for the customer.

The business is based in Sale and services customers across Gippsland. When you book, you are dealing directly with the operator rather than a large franchise network.

CG Mobile Detailing is fully insured and uses professional-grade detailing products and equipment.
