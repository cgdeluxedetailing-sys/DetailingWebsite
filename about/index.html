About

Hi, I'm Connor Garvey — the founder and sole operator of CG Mobile Detailing.

I started CG Mobile Detailing when I was 18 years old with one goal in mind to build something I was genuinely proud of, doing work I actually love.

I've had a passion for cars for as long as I can remember. There's something about taking a vehicle that's been neglected, weathered, or just tired from daily life and transforming it into something that looks better than the day it left the dealership. That feeling never gets old. And it's why I show up to every single job with the same care and attention, whether it's a daily driver or a weekend pride and joy.

CG Mobile Detailing was never just about making money. It was about building something real a business with a reputation worth having, in a community worth serving. Gippsland is home. These are my neighbours, my community, and the people I want to do right by every single time.

What makes CG different?

I'm not a franchise. I'm not a faceless company. When you book with CG Mobile Detailing, you get me, Connor, at your door with professional-grade products, genuine care for your vehicle, and a standard I refuse to drop.

I use only premium, industry-leading products on every job. I take my time. I don't rush. And I won't hand your car back until I'm personally satisfied with the result.

Every vehicle is treated like it's my own because I know how much yours means to you.

I come to you at your home, your workplace, or wherever suits you best across Sale, Traralgon, Bairnsdale, and throughout Gippsland.

Whether you're after a regular maintenance clean, a full transformation, a pre-sale detail to maximise your car's value, or long-term ceramic coating protection CG Mobile Detailing has you covered.

This is only the beginning. I'm building CG Mobile Detailing for the long term and every car I detail is another step toward becoming Gippsland's most trusted name in mobile detailing.

Thanks for being here. I'd love to take care of your car.

— Connor Garvey
Founder, CG Mobile Detailing
