Hi, I'm Connor Garvey.

I'm the founder and sole operator of CG Mobile Detailing.

I started CG Mobile Detailing when I was 18 years old with one goal in mind: to build something I was genuinely proud of, doing work I actually love.

I've had a passion for cars for as long as I can remember. There's something about taking a vehicle that's been neglected, weathered, or just tired from daily life and transforming it into something that looks better than the day it left the dealership. That feeling never gets old.

And it's why I show up to every single job with the same care and attention, whether it's a daily driver or a weekend pride and joy.
