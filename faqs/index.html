FAQ

Questions before you book?

Practical answers about mobile detailing, services, appointments and what to expect.

Do you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. CG Mobile Detailing is mobile by design and services suitable customer locations across the service area.

What do I need to provide?

Professional equipment is brought to the job. Access to power and water helps the detail run efficiently.

How long does a detail take?

Timing depends on the service and vehicle condition. Some appointments are around an hour while larger or more involved work takes longer.

What is the Ultra Valet?

It is the premium full-vehicle service combining the interior and exterior detailing processes for a complete reset.

Can you detail 4WDs and tradie vehicles?

The business services a range of vehicle types. Mention heavy use, unusual dimensions or specific areas needing attention when booking.

Do you detail boats?

Yes. Boat detailing is available across Gippsland and services are priced by metre.

How do I book?

Use the online booking page to open the existing Square booking system, or call 0436 288 675 if you need help choosing a service.

Do you charge a travel fee?

The previous website stated that travel was included within a standard service area and that a fee could apply for jobs 40km or more from Sale. Confirm the current travel arrangement when booking if your location is outside the core area.

What payment methods do you accept?

The existing website stated cash, bank transfer and card payments on the day, with payment due upon completion. Confirm current booking-specific requirements when using Square.

What is the cancellation policy?

The existing policy asks for at least 24 hours notice when cancelling or rescheduling. Late cancellations and no-shows may incur a fee.

Are you insured?

Yes. CG Mobile Detailing is presented as fully insured.
