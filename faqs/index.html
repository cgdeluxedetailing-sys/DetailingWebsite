FAQs

Everything you need to know before booking with CG Mobile Detailing.

General Questions

What is mobile detailing and how does it work?

Mobile detailing means we come to you — no drop-offs, no waiting rooms, no inconvenience. I bring professional equipment, products and water needed directly to your home, workplace or wherever your car is parked.

Where do you service?

CG Mobile Detailing operates across Gippsland including Sale, Traralgon, Bairnsdale, Maffra, Heyfield and surrounding areas. Not sure if we cover your area? Send us a message.

Do I need to be home during the detail?

Not at all. As long as we have safe access to your vehicle and a nearby power and water source, you don't need to be present.

Do you bring your own water and power?

For most jobs I can work with access to a standard garden tap and outdoor power point. If your location has limited access, get in touch before booking.

How long does each service take?

Interior Valet: approximately 2–3 hours. Exterior Valet: approximately 1.5–2.5 hours. Ultra Valet: approximately 3–4.5 hours. Showroom: approximately 4–6 hours. Pre-Sale: approximately 3–4.5 hours. Ceramic Coating: approximately 4–8 hours depending on vehicle size and condition.

Services & Results

What's the difference between a regular wash and a professional detail?

A regular car wash removes surface dirt. A professional detail goes several layers deeper, including paint decontamination, bonded iron and tar removal, deep interior cleaning and finishing work.

My car is really dirty inside — is that okay?

Absolutely. Whether it's built-up grime, pet hair, food spills or a vehicle that hasn't been cleaned properly in a long time, we've seen it all. No judgement, just results.

Can you remove pet hair?

Yes. Pet hair removal is included as part of the interior detailing process. Heavily affected vehicles may require additional time.

Can detailing remove scratches?

Light surface scratches and swirl marks can often be significantly improved. Deep scratches through the clear coat or paint may require paint correction and can be quoted separately.

How long will my detail last?

Interior Valet: 4–8 weeks. Exterior Valet: 2–4 weeks. Wax or sealant: 2–4 months. Ceramic Coating: 2–5 years depending on product and maintenance.

What is ceramic coating?

Ceramic coating is a professional-grade liquid polymer that bonds to the vehicle's paintwork and creates a protective layer designed to repel water, UV rays, dirt and contaminants while making the vehicle easier to maintain.

Do you detail boats and caravans?

Yes. CG Mobile Detailing services boats, caravans and other recreational vehicles across Gippsland. Get in touch with your vehicle details for a custom quote.

Standard bookings do not require a deposit. Ceramic coating bookings may require a deposit to secure the appointment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Do you offer discounts for multiple vehicles?

Yes. If you have more than one vehicle at the same location, get in touch for a package price.
