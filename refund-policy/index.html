Refund Policy

Information about bookings, cancellations, service concerns and refunds.

Bookings and cancellations

Customers are asked to provide at least 24 hours notice when cancelling or rescheduling. Late cancellations and no-shows may incur a fee because appointment time has been reserved specifically for the vehicle.

Service concerns

If you are unhappy with an aspect of the service, contact CG Mobile Detailing as soon as possible so the concern can be assessed and, where appropriate, rectified.

Refunds

Refunds are considered case by case depending on the circumstances of the service and payment. Completed services are not automatically refundable simply because a customer changes their mind after completion.

Contact

For questions about a payment, cancellation or service concern, contact help@cgmobiledetailing.com or 0436 288 675.
