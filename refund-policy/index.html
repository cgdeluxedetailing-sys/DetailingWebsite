Refund Policy

Information about payments, cancellations and service concerns.

Bookings and cancellations

We ask customers to provide at least 24 hours notice when cancelling or rescheduling an appointment. Late cancellations and no-shows may incur a fee because appointment time has been reserved specifically for your vehicle.

Service concerns

If you are unhappy with an aspect of your service, please contact CG Mobile Detailing as soon as possible so the concern can be assessed and, where appropriate, rectified.

Refunds

Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis depending on the circumstances of the service and payment. Services that have been completed are not automatically refundable simply because a customer changes their mind after completion.

Contact

For questions about a payment, cancellation or service concern, contact help@cgmobiledetailing.com or 0436 288 675.
